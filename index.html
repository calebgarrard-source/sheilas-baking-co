    From a bakery bus to Georgia Avenue

    Sheila Martin built her first bakery herself, on wheels. This is the short version.

    1. Pennsylvania

      Sheila has been in the business of baking since she was a teenager.

    2. Waynesboro, Georgia

      She moves south to learn the trade, at Wagon Barn Market and at Pineland Bakery.

    3. An old bus

      Her brother finds the bus. He and their father do the mechanical work. Sheila designs it, paints it and builds the counters herself. It takes about nine months.

    4. 2018

      Sheila's Baking Co. opens on wheels, inspired by Miss Miller's, a Tennessee bakery in an old school bus. Doughnuts and apple fritters are the signatures.

    5. Georgia Avenue

      The storefront opens in North Augusta in the middle of a pandemic, and stays open. The bus keeps going out.

    6. Today

      4.9 stars from 491 Google reviews. Still no mixes.
